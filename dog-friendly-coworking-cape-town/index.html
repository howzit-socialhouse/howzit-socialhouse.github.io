Dog-friendly coworking in Cape Town, in Sea Point

Well-behaved dogs are welcome at Howzit?, a coworking house in Sea Point: under your desk or out in the garden while you work. We call it co-barking. From 1 December 2026; book a visit now.

How co-barking works

Your dog comes to work with you and you keep an eye on them: garden time, a water bowl and dog-loving coworkers. The Promenade is a five-minute walk for a lunchtime stroll.

House rules: what “well-behaved” means

  • Vaccinations up to date (please bring proof)
  • Friendly with people and other dogs
  • House-trained
  • One dog per member unless agreed in advance
  • A few dogs at a time at most, so please book ahead
  • You remain responsible for your dog

We meet every dog first, so mention yours when you book a visit.

FAQ

Can I bring my dog to coworking in Cape Town?

Yes. At Howzit? in Sea Point, bringing your dog is free on an annual hot desk or private office membership. Everyone else pays a pet fee of R95 a day or R950 a month per dog, and you look after your dog.

How much is a day pass with a dog?

R490 in summer and R420 in winter: the day pass plus the R95 pet fee.

Can you look after my dog if I am not working at the house?

Yes. Drop your dog off for pet sitting, from R120 an hour or R380 for a full day, and collect them later. No membership needed.

Are all dogs welcome?

Well-behaved dogs are: vaccinations up to date, friendly with people and other dogs, and house-trained. We meet every dog first and keep it to a few dogs at a time.

Can I bring two dogs?

One dog per member unless agreed in advance. If we agree a second dog, it is +50% on the pet fee or pet-sitting rate.

Where is Howzit?

In Sea Point, Cape Town, about five minutes’ walk from the Promenade. We share exact directions when you book a visit.
