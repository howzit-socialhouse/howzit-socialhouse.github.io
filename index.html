Off chasing summer? Leave your stuff with us.

Spending the Cape Town winter somewhere sunnier? Store your suitcases, boxes and bits safely at the house until you're back.

Per bag or boxR295 / monthAny time of year, month to month
Winter season bundleR2,450 / seasonUp to 2 bags or boxes, 1 May–31 Oct. Extra items R895 each.
How it works & good to know
  1. Tell us what you're storing: roughly how many bags or boxes, and your dates away.
  2. Drop off at the house: pack and label your things, and we log each item with you.
  3. Go explore: your belongings stay in a dedicated, lockable storage space at the house.
  4. Welcome back! Let us know when you land, then pop in to collect (and stay for a coffee).

No food, liquids, valuables, cash or hazardous items. Size and weight limits apply per item; we confirm them when you enquire. Please keep your own travel or contents insurance. You get our simple storage terms to agree before drop-off. Members get 20% off storage. Prices in ZAR, incl. VAT where applicable.

How it works
  1. Book through the contact form (choose “Luggage drop”) and we confirm your slot.

Bring your dog to work. We'll mind them when you can't.

Your dog can lie under your desk or hang out in the garden while you work. Free on an annual hot desk or private office membership; everyone else pays R95 per day or R950 per month per dog.

  • Pet sitting, per hour (1 hour min.)R120
  • Half day (up to 4 hours)R250
  • Full day (up to 9 hours)R380
  • Monthly weekdays (up to 20 full days)R3,800
Details & house rules

Pet sitting: we look after your dog at the house while you're busy, or while you're not at the house at all. Supervision, fresh water, garden play and short walks. No membership needed. Per dog, any size; a second dog is +50%.

Yearly members: free pet sitting for one dog during opening hours (subject to space).

“Well-behaved” means: vaccinations up to date (please bring proof), friendly with people and other dogs, and house-trained. One dog per member unless agreed in advance, a few dogs at a time at most, so please book ahead. You remain liable for your dog. We meet every dog first.

Prices in ZAR, incl. VAT where applicable. Launch prices, subject to change.
