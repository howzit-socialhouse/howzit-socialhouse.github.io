How the bag drop in Sea Point works

  1. Book through the contact form (choose “Luggage drop”) or WhatsApp, and we confirm your slot.
  2. Drop off during opening hours. We share them when we confirm.
  3. Sealed & tagged: each bag gets a numbered tag and you keep the matching stub.
  4. Collect during opening hours with your stub.

Opening hours

We're setting launch hours now and share the drop-off and collection hours when we confirm your booking. Exact directions to the house in Sea Point come with your confirmation too.

What you can't store

No valuables, cash, laptops, passports, food or liquids.

FAQ

How much is luggage storage in Sea Point?

From R65 per small bag for up to 4 hours. A large checked suitcase is R80 for up to 4 hours or R120 for a day.

Do I need an app?

No. Book through our contact form or WhatsApp. No app, no platform fees.

What can't I leave?

No valuables, cash, laptops, passports, food or liquids.

Can I store a surfboard or bike box?

Yes, odd-size items are R110 for up to 4 hours, R160 a day and R130 each extra day.
