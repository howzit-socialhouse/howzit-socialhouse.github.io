Coworking in Cape Town: why Sea Point?

Sea Point sits on Cape Town's Atlantic Seaboard, between Lion's Head and the Atlantic Ocean, next door to Green Point and Three Anchor Bay. It's one of the city's most walkable neighbourhoods, so a coworking space here gives you the city and the sea in the same day.

  • The Promenade: a 5-minute walk from the house to the Sea Point Promenade and the ocean.
  • Coffee and lunch: coffee shops, bakeries and lunch spots less than 5 minutes away.
  • The CBD and V&A Waterfront: a short drive away, and MyCiTi bus routes serve Sea Point.

If you're comparing coworking spaces in Cape Town, Howzit? is the one in a house: a pool, a garden and a braai, with fast fibre for the serious work. We share exact directions when you book a visit.

A quick rule of thumb

  • Here for a day or two? Take a day pass.
  • Coming in a few days a week? The 10-day pack (R2,950, valid 60 days) gives you flexibility at the same price all year.
  • Working every weekday? Weekly or monthly works out cheaper, especially at winter rates.
  • Staying in Cape Town for the long run? The yearly membership is the best value.

Yearly terms in plain English

A yearly membership or 12-month office lease is a 12-month contract paid monthly at a fixed rate. Leaving early? Give 2 months' written notice and pay back the yearly discount you've had so far, nothing more. After 12 months you can renew yearly or carry on month to month.

How to store a suitcase in Cape Town over winter

  1. Tell us what you're storing: send a quick enquiry with roughly how many bags or boxes, and your dates away.
  2. Drop off at the house: pack and label your things, bring them over, and we log each item with you.
  3. Go explore: your belongings stay in a dedicated storage space at the house while you travel.
  4. Welcome back! Let us know when you land, then pop in to collect your things (and stay for a coffee).

What's included

  • A dedicated, lockable storage area in the house
  • Item list logged at drop-off
  • Flexible collection dates when you're back
  • 20% off for Howzit? members

Good to know (including insurance)

  • No food, liquids, valuables, cash or hazardous items.
  • Size and weight limits apply per item; we confirm them when you enquire.
  • Please keep your own travel or contents insurance for stored items.
  • You get our simple storage terms to agree before drop-off.

FAQ

How much is a coworking day pass in Sea Point?

A Howzit? day pass is R395 in summer (November to April) and R325 in winter (May to October), including fibre wifi, coffee, and pool and garden access.

Where exactly is it?

Howzit? is in Sea Point, Cape Town, about 5 minutes' walk from the Promenade. We share exact directions when you book a visit.

Is there parking?

Yes, 2 parking bays available.

How does the 10-day pack work?

It's 10 days of hot desking for R2,950, valid for 60 days, at the same price all year.

What's the cheapest long-term option?

The yearly membership: R2,750 a month on a 12-month contract, paid monthly. That's R33,000 a year instead of R39,000 on monthly desks.

How much is a private office in Sea Point?

Office 1 or 2 (for teams of up to 6): R18,000 a month on a 12-month lease, or R20,000 month to month. Office 3 (the big room with its own en-suite bathroom, up to 8 people): R22,000 a month on a 12-month lease, or R24,000 month to month. Smaller teams of 2–4 are welcome too.

Can I rent an office just for a day?

Yes, if one is free that day: a day office is R950 for up to 3 people.

Can I bring my dog to coworking?

Yes. It is free for members on an annual hot desk or private office membership. Everyone else pays a pet fee of R95 a day or R950 a month per dog, and you look after your dog.

Can you look after my dog if I am not working at the house?

Yes. Drop your dog off for pet sitting (from R120 an hour, R380 a full day) and collect them later; no membership needed.

How much is pet sitting?

From R120 an hour, R250 for a half day, R380 for a full day or R3,800 for monthly weekdays. A second dog is +50%.

How much does nomad storage cost?

R295 per bag or box per month, or R2,450 for the winter season bundle (up to 2 items, 1 May to 31 October). Extra items in the bundle are R895 each.

Is my stuff insured?

Please keep your own travel or contents insurance for stored items.
